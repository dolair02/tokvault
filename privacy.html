Privacy Policy

TokVault

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

TokVault (“TokVault”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is designed with a privacy-first architecture. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the TokVault app for Android, the TokVault app for iPhone and the TokVault web app.

1. Information We Collect & Store

  • Saved links and collections (local-first): By default, all video links, custom folders, tags and personal notes you save in TokVault are stored on your device only.
  • Optional cloud backup account: If you choose to create a cloud backup account, we store your email address, an account ID and the library you back up (your saved links, folders, tags and notes) with our hosting provider, Supabase, so you can restore your library on another device. You do not need an account to use the app.
  • Subscription status: Subscriptions and free trials are sold and charged by Google Play in the Android app (by Apple in the iPhone app, and by Stripe on the web). We receive only whether a subscription is active and which plan it is. We never see or store your payment card details.

2. Link Previews & Third-Party Content

When you paste a public TikTok video link, the app asks TikTok’s public oEmbed service for the title, creator handle and thumbnail so it can show a preview. If TikTok does not answer, the app asks Microlink (api.microlink.io), a link-preview service, for the same public information. These services receive the link you pasted and your device’s IP address; they do not receive your account or the rest of your library. Thumbnails are loaded from the servers that host them (for example TikTok’s content network). When you tap play on a video inside the app, the app shows TikTok’s embedded player (www.tiktok.com), which is run by TikTok under TikTok’s own privacy policy and receives your device’s IP address.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To save, organize, tag and search your saved links.
  • To back up and restore your library when you are signed in.
  • To confirm your TokVault Pro subscription status and to answer support requests.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, rent or trade your personal data to third parties or advertisers. TokVault contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and does no cross-app tracking. Data goes only to the service providers needed to run the features above: Supabase (cloud backup hosting), Google Play (billing), TikTok (link previews and the embedded video player) and Microlink (link previews).

5. Export & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data at all times:

  • Export: You can export your whole library as a portable JSON file at any time from the Backup tab in the app.
  • Delete your account and cloud data: When you are signed in, open Settings and tap Delete account & data, then Email my deletion request. You can also email dolair01@gmail.com from the email address of your account. We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days. Step-by-step instructions and what is deleted.
  • Data on your device: Links saved only on your device stay there until you clear them in the app or uninstall it.

6. Data Retention

Data on your device stays there until you remove it. Cloud backup data is kept while your account exists and is deleted within 30 days after we receive and confirm your deletion request. Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google’s policies.

7. Security

Information sent between the app and our cloud backup provider travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Children

TokVault is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at dolair01@gmail.com.
