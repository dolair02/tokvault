Privacy Policy

TokVault - Save Video Links

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

TokVault - Save Video Links ("TokVault", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is designed with a privacy-first architecture. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the TokVault mobile application and web application.

1. Information We Collect & Store

  • Saved Video Links & Collections (Local-First): By default, all video links, custom folders, tags, and personal notes you save in TokVault are stored locally on your device.
  • Optional Cloud Sync Account: If you choose to create an optional cloud backup account, we store your email address and encrypted vault data via Supabase so you can sync and restore your saved links across your devices.
  • Subscription & Billing Information: Subscriptions and free trials are processed securely through Apple In-App Purchases (App Store), Google Play Billing, or Stripe. We do not store your credit card numbers or payment credentials on our servers.

2. Public Video Metadata (oEmbed)

When you paste a public TikTok video URL into TokVault, the app requests publicly available metadata (such as video title, creator handle, and thumbnail preview) directly from TikTok's public oEmbed endpoint to help organize your link.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To save, organize, tag, and search your bookmarked video links.
  • To synchronize and back up your library across devices when signed in.
  • To verify your TokVault Pro subscription status and provide customer support.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Tracking

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertisers. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

5. Data Export & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data at all times:

  • You can export your entire library as a portable JSON file at any time from the Backup tab inside the app.
  • You can clear your local vault or delete your cloud account and all associated cloud data directly inside the app or by emailing us at dolair01@gmail.com.
