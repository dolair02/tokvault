Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save a TikTok video link to TokVault?
Tap "Share -> Copy Link" on any TikTok video, open TokVault, and tap "+ Save Link". TokVault automatically fetches the video title, creator handle, hashtags, and embedded player preview.
How do I restore my TokVault Pro subscription on a new device?
Open TokVault and tap "Restore Purchases" on the TokVault Pro screen, or sign in with your TokVault Cloud account under the Backup tab.
How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
For Apple App Store subscriptions, open your iPhone/iPad Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > TokVault. You can cancel anytime at least 24 hours before the end of your 3-day free trial or billing period.
How do I back up or delete my data?
Open the "Backup" tab inside TokVault to sync with your encrypted cloud account, export a portable JSON backup file, or permanently delete your vault data.
