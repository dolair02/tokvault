Account deletion

Delete your TokVault account

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This page explains how to ask us to delete your TokVault account and the data stored for it.

How to request deletion

In the app (signed-in accounts):

  1. Open TokVault and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account & data.
  3. Tap Email my deletion request and send the email that opens (it already contains your account email).

Without the app: email dolair01@gmail.com from the email address of your TokVault account with the subject “TokVault account deletion request”.

We confirm your request by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and the email address we hold for it.
  • Your account ID and your cloud backup: the saved links, folders, tags and notes you backed up.

What is kept

  • Nothing about you is kept in our cloud backup after the deletion is complete.
  • Links saved only on your device stay on your device until you clear them in the app (Backup tab) or uninstall TokVault.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google’s policies. Deleting your TokVault account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play (Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > TokVault).

Only want to delete some data?

You can delete individual links, folders or notes inside the app at any time, and export your library as a JSON file from the Backup tab first. See the Privacy Policy for details.
